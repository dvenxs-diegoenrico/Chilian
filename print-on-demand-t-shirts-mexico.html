Questions

What people ask most

What is the minimum?

One piece. There is no minimum in print on demand.

What does the price include?

The T-shirt, one full-color DTG print up to the size you choose, setup, quality check and individual packing, VAT and payment fee. An extra print adds its price; shipping is separate.

What file do I need?

A 300 DPI PNG with a transparent background is ideal. We also accept JPG, SVG and WebP, and we warn you if the resolution is low.

Can I order different designs in one order?

Yes. The price per piece drops with the total pieces, even if each one has a different design.

Can I use the free designs?

Yes. They are public-domain works taken from Wikimedia Commons; you are responsible for not combining them with protected brands or text.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes, we ship nationally and internationally. Outside Mexico your advisor quotes freight and export documents; the final price is confirmed in pesos.
